01 / Who we are

Curiosity with
workshop grit.

AeroBMSCE is an aerospace, aeromodelling and unmanned systems club at BMS College of Engineering.

The team’s work brings students together around practical engineering: shaping an idea, making a system, testing it, and learning from what happens next.
